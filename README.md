## Sergi Fernandez

Product Manager in Sweden. I work on digital products where the hard part is not writing more requirements, but turning ambiguity into decisions that teams can ship.

I am strongest when the problem sits across users, data, operations, commercial logic, and technical constraints. That is where product work gets interesting: weak signal, too many stakeholders, incomplete evidence, real delivery limits, and a business case that still has to hold.

My background combines product management, market research, data analysis, and enough technical fluency to work close to engineering without pretending to be an engineer.

I am opinionated about how products should feel. Strong products make the next step obvious, reduce interpretation load, and map directly to the workflow a user is trying to complete. Data helps define the problem; product taste shapes the experience.

I am currently deepening my AI and technical workflow practice: AI-assisted product discovery, agentic workflows, frontend fundamentals, and automation. Expect the occasional experiment here; the anchor is still product judgment, not technology theater.
